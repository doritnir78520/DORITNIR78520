Questions, answered.

A few helpful details about our concept collections and demonstration website.

Can I purchase products on this website?

Not yet. DORITNIR78520 is a concept storefront. Products, prices and the shopping bag are for demonstration only.

Are the prices real?

No. Prices are illustrative and do not represent live offers or available inventory.

Does the shopping bag process payments?

No. The bag stores a local demo selection in your browser only. There is no checkout or payment processing.

Can I place a custom furniture order?

Custom orders are not currently available. Any future services will be announced through official channels.

Does the contact form send my message?

No. The form is a front-end demonstration and does not transmit your message.

Where are the images from?

The site uses externally hosted Unsplash imagery as editorial and product concept references. They are not verified photos of products in stock.
